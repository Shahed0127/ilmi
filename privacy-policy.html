ILMI (عِلْمِي) – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. App owner: Muhammad Shahidul Islam Shahed.

What we collect

ILMI does not require an account and does not collect, sell or share personal information. There are no ads and no analytics.

Data stored on your device

Your exam results, favourite questions, wrong-answer list and any questions you add are saved only in your device's local storage. This data never leaves your device. Clearing the app or browser data removes it.

Third-party services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive your IP address when fonts are requested. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Children

ILMI is intended for exam candidates and students and is not directed at children under 13.
